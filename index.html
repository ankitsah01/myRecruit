About MyRecruit

A Recruitment Partner You Can Trust

MyRecruit Ltd is a Mauritius-based recruitment agency specialising in the sourcing, screening and placement of overseas workers across key industries throughout Mauritius.

We work directly with Mauritian employers to understand their workforce requirements and coordinate the identification, review and recruitment of suitable non-citizen candidates — managing the process from initial sourcing through to work permit coordination and worker arrival.

Our approach is built on transparency, professionalism and a genuine commitment to responsible recruitment practices that protect both employer and worker interests.

How The Process Works

A clear, compliant, and transparent 5-step roadmap engineered for both employers and overseas job seekers — from first consultation to verified placement.

01
Requirements Phase

Tell Us Your Requirement

Submit your precise workforce needs, role specifications, required skills, and expected arrival timeline.

Job profile analysis • Headcount planning • Salary benchmarking
02
International Talent Sourcing

We Source & Rigorously Screen

Our sourcing network across accredited partner regions reviews background, qualifications, and past performance.

Skill competency assessment • Criminal background check • Trade verification
03
Candidate Selection

Shortlisting & Employer Interview

Curated shortlist of pre-screened candidates presented with video profiles and interviews coordinated seamlessly.

Video interview coordination • Technical evaluation • Employer choice confirmation
04
Statutory Compliance

Documentation & Work Permit Support

Complete administrative management for Mauritius Work Permit applications, attestation, and medical clearances.

Ministry of Labour liaison • Medical fitness vetting • Flight logistics arrangement
05
Deployment & Onboarding

Worker Arrival & On-Site Handover

Worker arrives in Mauritius, airport reception coordination, and orientation to seamlessly integrate into your operations.

Airport pick-up assistance • Orientation briefing • Post-placement check-ins

Our Commitment

Recruitment With Transparency & Responsibility

Responsible recruitment is central to how we operate. We are committed to a process that is transparent, fair and protective of all parties involved — both employer and worker.

Our principles are built around honesty, verified information and a clear understanding of rights and responsibilities at every stage of the recruitment process.

Candidate Verification

We review candidate identity, documentation and relevant experience before presenting any individual to an employer.

Clear Employment Terms

Workers should understand their employment terms, conditions and responsibilities before accepting any placement.

No Worker Placement Fees

Workers are not charged recruitment placement fees. Applying to MyRecruit is entirely free for all candidates.
